## Behaviour
- **What can be reported**: a published listing, a review shown on a published listing, an active member. Anything else (missing, malformed id, draft/unpublished/deleted listing, suspended member) is the same 404. A message cannot be reported yet (400 "Messages cannot be reported yet"): the model already allows `message`, so P11 needs no migration.
- **Not your own**: the listing's host, the review's author and the member themselves get 403. A review without an author (old data) can be reported by anyone.
- **Reasons**: spam, scam, inaccurate, offensive, unsafe, privacy, other. Anything else is refused. "Something else" needs at least 10 characters of details; details are plain text up to 1000 characters, trimmed, refused (not cut) when longer, shown escaped.
- **No duplicates**: the friendly check (`exists`) answers 409 `already_reported` ("You already have an open report about this..."), and the unique index decides when two requests race (verified with 8 parallel requests in `reports.db.test.js`). The person is sent to "Your reports" with that message. After a report is closed, the same person can report the same thing again.
- **Rate limiting**: 5 `POST /reports` per minute per signed-in member (in memory, per server process), and 20 reports per 24 hours per member counted in the database (429 `report_limit`). Other members are unaffected.
- **Who reports** is the session's user, never a form field; status, owner and label are set by the server (a forged `reporter`, `status`, `targetOwner`, `targetLabel` is ignored: tested).
- **The reporter sees the status**: Received (open), Looked at (resolved), Closed (dismissed), each with one plain sentence. Nothing about who looked at it or what was decided. The reported person is not told who reported them (the form says so).
- Report links are shown to visitors who are not signed in too: following one asks them to log in and returns to the form. They are hidden for the owner of the thing and for accounts that cannot report.
- The CSRF check stays global with no exception for `POST /reports` (tested).
