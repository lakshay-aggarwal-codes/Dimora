## Decisions
- **Publish is here, minimally**: the card says "cannot skip to publish", so step 6 publishes a draft once steps 1-5 are finished and valid (draft -> published only). Unpublish, readiness details and the rest of the lifecycle are P04-T05.
- **Step completion is stored, not derived**: capacity, nights and times have defaults, so derived completeness would mark steps 3 and 5 done before the host opened them.
- **Street address is private**: only city and state reach the public `location` text. The exact point is still public until P06-T02 (approximate location).
- The old `/listings/new` form and `POST /listings` stay (tests and P04-T04 still use them); nothing links to the form any more.
