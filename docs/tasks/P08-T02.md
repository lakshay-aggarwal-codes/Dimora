## Behaviour
- **400** (bad input): `{error: {status, code, message, fields: {in?, out?, guests?}}}`. Every problem is listed under its field name.
- **200, `bookable: false`**: a valid request the rules or the ledger refuse: minimum/maximum stay, capacity, notice, cut-off, taken nights. `problems` has the reasons, `quote` is null. Taken nights are listed as dates only; a block, a reservation and a live hold read the same.
- **200, `bookable: true`**: `quote` plus `expectedTotalMinor`, the number checkout (task 51) must send back so `reservations.create` can detect a changed price.
- **422** when the listing has no valid nightly price; **404** for a listing the caller may not see.
- The panel shows "Choose your dates", then "Checking the price...", then lines and total, or the reasons. Reserve is a link that is disabled (`aria-disabled`, no `href`, not focusable) until the server answers `bookable: true`.
- Amounts are only formatted in the browser (`Intl`, integer paise divided by 100 for display). Nothing is calculated there.

## Limitations
- Reserve points to `/listings/:id/checkout?in&out&guests`, which is built in task 51 (P08-T03); until then it is a 404.
- The date picker does not grey out dates that notice or the same-day cut-off would refuse; the panel shows the reason after the dates are chosen.
- The quote is a preview: `reservations.create` prices again at booking time.
- The rate limit is per IP and in memory (one process).

## Manual checks (owner, in a browser)
1. Open a published listing; the panel shows a guests select and "Choose your dates to see the price."; Reserve is greyed and cannot be tabbed to.
2. Pick dates: the breakdown appears, the total equals the sum of the lines, Reserve becomes active.
3. Change the guests: the price refreshes. Pick a stay shorter than the minimum: the reason appears and Reserve is disabled again.
4. Turn off JavaScript: the panel and the picker stay hidden and the notice shows.
5. With a screen reader, the breakdown is announced when it changes (it is a polite live region).
