## What it does
1. Checkout (`POST /listings/:id/checkout`) with `SIMULATED_PAYMENTS` on (default) creates the booking as **`awaiting_payment`** and redirects to `/trips/:id/pay`. The nights are held in the ledger for `PAYMENT_HOLD_MINUTES` (default 15). The host is told nothing yet.
2. The pay page (`views/payments/pay.ejs`) shows a **TEST MODE: no money moves** banner, the stay, the amount, the time left and one **Pay (simulated)** button. No card, name, expiry or CVV field exists. A hidden key makes the click idempotent. With `SIMULATED_PAYMENT_OUTCOMES` on, a developer selector (succeeds / is declined / service error) appears.
3. `POST /trips/:id/pay` -> `services/payments.pay`: the attempt is stored first (`PaymentAttempt`), then the provider answers (`paymentProviders/simulated.js`, moves nothing). **Only on success** the booking moves on through the reservation service's compare-and-set: `confirmed` (instant book) or `pending` (the host's 24 hours start now; ledger hold moved with `extendHold`). The host's notification (`reservation.booked` / `reservation.requested`) is sent at that moment.
4. A refusal (declined, service error) changes nothing on the booking: **the nights stay held until the payment hold ends**, then the sweeper expires it ("The payment was not completed in time.") and releases the nights. No refund is asked for (nothing was paid). The guest may try again with a new key while the hold runs.
5. A paid booking that is declined, expires or is cancelled gets **one** `refunded_simulated` record (`payments.refundRelease`, called by the reservation service; a guest cancellation records the amount the cancellation policy worked out, never more than the charge) and `paymentStatus: refunded_simulated`. A failing refund record never undoes the cancellation.

## Limitations and decisions
- A guest cannot abandon an unpaid booking early; the nights free up when the hold ends (a cancel for `awaiting_payment` was left out on purpose: nothing was paid, and the refund code assumes a paid or pending booking).
- The host's calendar shows an unpaid booking's nights as "booked" for up to 15 minutes, with no guest name and no link (`hostCalendar.js`; test added). Account deletion is not blocked by an unpaid booking.
- With no `payments` key in the config (older test configs) checkout books directly, exactly as before; the real app always has one (default on). `SIMULATED_PAYMENTS=false` is the switch back.
- The outcome selector is a testing tool; the env check warns when it is on in production.
- `checkout`'s repeat-submit path (a paid booking found again by its key) still flashes the old "no payment was taken" wording.

## Manual checks (owner)
1. `npm run dev` with `SIMULATED_PAYMENT_OUTCOMES=true`. Book a listing: you land on "Pay (simulated)" with the TEST MODE banner and no card fields.
2. Choose "Is declined", press the button: red message, dates still held until the time shown. Press again with "Succeeds": the booking page shows it booked (instant) or sent to the host, and the host's request list now has it.
3. Press the button twice quickly: one charge (check `paymentattempts` in Compass).
4. Set `PAYMENT_HOLD_MINUTES=1`, book and wait: the booking turns to "Payment not completed", the sweeper expires it and the dates open up.
5. Have the host decline a paid request: one `refunded_simulated` record appears and the booking's `paymentStatus` says so.
