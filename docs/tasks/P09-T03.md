## The rules
Cut-offs are measured from **check-in = the check-in date at the listing's check-in time, on the listing's own clock** (`listingSnapshot.timezone`, default Asia/Kolkata; "00:00" if the snapshot has no valid time). "Until X before check-in" is inclusive: cancelling exactly at the cut-off instant is still in the earlier tier. All amounts are integer paise. "First night" is the stay's average nightly price (`avgPerNightMinor`, the nights only, no fees).

| Who / when | Refund | Rule id |
|---|---|---|
| The host cancels (confirmed booking) | everything | `host_cancelled` |
| The guest withdraws a request the host has not answered | everything | `request_withdrawn` |
| The guest, stay already started (`now >=` check-in) | nothing | `stay_started` |
| The guest, listing without (or with an unknown) policy | nothing promised | `no_policy` |
| **flexible**: at least 24 h before check-in | everything | `flexible_free` |
| flexible: later | everything except the first night | `flexible_late` |
| **moderate**: at least 5 days before | everything | `moderate_free` |
| moderate: later | everything except the first night and half of the other nights' price (the kept half is rounded up, so a part-paise goes to the host) | `moderate_late` |
| **strict**: within 48 h of booking, check-in at least 14 days away | everything | `strict_grace` |
| strict: at least 7 days before | half of the total (rounded down) | `strict_half` |
| strict: later | nothing | `strict_none` |

Cleaning fee, service fee and taxes are refunded or kept as part of "everything / the total": only the first night and the half of the other nights' price are ever withheld by the flexible and moderate rules. The booking time for the strict grace period is `createdAt` (a request counts from when it was sent), falling back to the first status-history entry; without either there is no grace period.

## Flow
- **Guest**: `/trips/:id` shows, for a pending or confirmed booking whose stay is not over, what cancelling now would return (and until when it stays free), a reason box (optional) and a confirm button. `POST /trips/:id/cancel` -> `cancelBooking` -> redirect back to the booking page, which then shows who cancelled, the reason and the recorded refund.
- **Host**: each confirmed stay on `/host/reservations` has "Cancel this stay..." with a required reason and the consequences spelled out (full refund, counted on the public profile). A pending request is declined, not cancelled (409 `use_decline`). A missing reason shows the inbox again with a 400 and the message; a booking that changed meanwhile shows a 409.
- **Who is cancelling comes from who the person is** (guest or host of that booking), never from the form (tested: forged `actor`, `to`, `refund` fields are ignored). Strangers, admins, suspended accounts, missing bookings and malformed ids all get the same 404.
- **Not after the stay is over**: from the day of check-out (listing's zone) on, 409 `not_cancellable`.
- **Exactly one winner**: the refund is computed from the status the person saw and stored by the compare-and-set that cancels; `expectStatus` makes a request that was accepted in between fail with a 409 instead of receiving the "free withdrawal" refund. Parallel host cancellations count once.
- **Host counter**: `$inc` after the cancellation succeeded, only for the winner. If that update fails the cancellation stands, the failure is logged, and the host sees no error (the count could then be one short; there is no retry).
- The nights are released by the existing transition (`releaseNights`), so the sweeper's invariants hold (tested with the invariant checker).
- **Nothing is paid back.** The record is `Reservation.refund`; `paymentStatus` is not changed (P16 will act on the record).

## Wording (one source of truth)
Checkout (task 51) and the booking page (task 52) used two different sets of sentences that contradicted each other (flexible: "not refunded" vs "first night not refunded"). They are now one: `CANCELLATION_TERMS` in `src/config/constants.js` (checkout's `cancellationText.js` re-exports it). I kept the task-52 rules and added the three general rules that the code applies to every policy: nothing is refunded once the stay has started, a request the host has not answered can be cancelled for free, and a host cancellation refunds everything. This **replaces the wording task 51 introduced** (moderate: "half back until 24 h before", strict: "half back until 7 days before"); existing bookings show the new text because the booking page reads the policy name, not a stored sentence. `tests/unit/refunds.test.js` checks that the thresholds in the sentences are the thresholds in the data.

## Deviations and limits
- Sentences changed (see above); the roadmap only fixes flexible's free window.
- "Within 48 hours of booking" is measured to the guest's request time, also for host-approval bookings.
- Admins cannot cancel a booking (no admin cancel flow is in the roadmap).
- A host cannot cancel a pending request (declining is the way); a guest can cancel at any time before check-out, even during the stay (nothing refunded then).
- The counter is not decremented if a cancellation were ever reversed (cancellations are final).
- The refund preview uses the server's clock at page load; the amount is recomputed when the button is pressed, so it can differ if a cut-off passes in between (the page says what is calculated "if you cancel now").
- Refund amounts apply to the whole total as a simulated payment; no partial-stay (leaving early) refunds.
