## Rules
- View = once per session per listing per day (session marks), India-time days.
- Not counted: bots (User-Agent heuristic), the host, `?preview=1`, non-public listings, requests without a session.
- Failures are logged and ignored; a page never fails because of a statistic.
- Conversion = bookings and requests created in the 30 days / views in the 30 days, shown only at 30+ views.
- Lifetime saves (`ListingStat.saves`) are net and clamped at 0; the daily saves count new saves only.

## Manual checks
1. `npm test`.
2. Open a published listing in a private window a few times: the host's `/host/listings/:id/stats` shows 1 view; a second private window makes 2; your own host account adds none.
3. Save and un-save a stay: "saved right now" goes 1 to 0, "times saved" stays 1.
4. Start a checkout (pick dates, Reserve): "Checkouts started" goes up once per day.

## Limitations
- Approximate by design: the bot check is a heuristic; clearing cookies counts again.
- Anonymous visitors receive a session cookie when their view is first counted.
- Rows older than 180 days are not rolled up yet; a few hundred rows per busy listing per year.
- Not shown: unique visitors, sources, other categories.
