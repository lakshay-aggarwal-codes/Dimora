# P08-T04 - Dynamic price rules (Q87)

Date: 2026-10-29 - No dependency, no migration (new optional fields). Dep: P08-T01 (done).

## Priority rules (also in `src/services/pricing.js` and on the form)
1. Seasonal rate covering the night replaces the nightly price (dates inclusive; max 5; no overlap; earlier start wins if stored data overlaps).
2. Weekend night (Fri/Sat): price from 1 plus `weekendBps`; in a season too. No markup: the older absolute weekend price applies to non-seasonal nights only.
3. Length discount on the nights subtotal: 28+ nights monthly %, 7+ weekly %, the larger one only, max 50%, not on cleaning.
4. Cleaning, then service fee and tax on (nights - discount + cleaning).
Lines: `nights`, `weekend_nights`, `season_nights`, `season_weekend_nights` (name + key), `length_discount` (negative), `cleaning`, `service_fee`, `tax`.

## Limitations
- The card said "weekend multiplier": implemented as a percentage increase (`weekendBps`); the older absolute weekend price is replaced when the step is saved.
- Seeded demo listings still use the older absolute weekend price (works; shows as % in the form).
- Reservations made before this change keep their stored lines; nothing is recalculated.
- No guest-facing note on the listing page about discounts; no per-season minimum stay.
