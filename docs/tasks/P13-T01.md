## The four tiles, and the definitions behind them
1. **Waiting for your answer** - pending reservations of this host whose hold has not run out (`holdExpiresAt` after now), and the soonest expiry. Link: `/host/reservations`.
2. **Check-ins and check-outs, next 7 days** - confirmed stays with check-in, and with check-out, from today through the sixth day ahead (a window of 7 dates, in Asia/Kolkata); the count and the next five of each, each linked to its booking page. Link: the confirmed list on `/host/reservations`.
3. **Estimated earnings** - the sum, over confirmed and completed stays that **start this month**, of the host-receivable amount of the stored price (`pricingSnapshot`). **Host receivable = the `nights`, `weekend_nights` and `cleaning` lines.** The guest service fee is Dimora's and the tax line is passed on, so neither is counted; an unreadable or missing price counts as 0, never as an error. The tile says "Estimate, no payouts": nothing is paid out and payments are simulated (ADR-009). A booking that is cancelled, declined or expired is not included. "Starts this month" is my reading of the card, which names no period; it keeps the tile from growing for ever and is stated on the page. Link: `/host/calendar`.
4. **Occupancy, next 30 nights** - `booked / (published listings x 30 - blocked)`, rounded to a whole percent, from the night ledger over the 30 nights starting today. Booked = a night held by a confirmed or completed reservation of this host; blocked = a host block (not available, so taken out of the denominator); a pending request counts as neither (it may be declined); a request hold that ran out counts as nothing. Drafts, unpublished and suspended listings are left out. With no published listing, or every night blocked, the tile shows a dash and says why instead of 0% or a division by zero. Link: `/host/calendar`.

A host with no listings sees an invitation to create one instead of four empty tiles.

## Limitations
- Up to 500 rows are read per tile query; a tile shows "N+" for requests when capped and the estimate would be low for a host with more than 500 stays in a month (not expected here).
- Earnings are an estimate in rupees only (the app prices in INR) and ignore cancellations that happen after confirmation (task 54 is not recorded as built); a cancelled stay leaves the figure by its status.
- Occupancy ignores listings' minimum stay and partial months; it is nights, nothing more.
- No comparison with last month and no chart, by design.
- Tiles are for the signed-in member's own listings; there is no per-listing breakdown (P13-T03 is listing stats).
