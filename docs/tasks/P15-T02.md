## Decisions and limits
- **Map layer without item coordinates.** The roadmap asks for a map layer "at the approximate centre with distances relative to it". P15-T01 stores no coordinates for places (on purpose), so places cannot be plotted. The layer is one dashed circle per category around the approximate centre, at the distance of the nearest place. This also satisfies the privacy rule (no per-item coordinates when fewer than 3 places are within 500 m) by construction.
- Distances shown are rounded again to the nearest 50 m (stored ones are rounded to 10 m).
- The circle needs the detail map to load; if the map fails, the checkbox never appears and the lists still work.
- Visitors see stored data of any age; the date is shown. P15-T01's retry job and host button keep it fresh.
- The section also appears on a host's own preview of an unpublished listing (same data).

## Manual checks (browser)
1. Open a published listing that has nearby data: "What's around" appears under the map with tabs; unpublished/never-fetched listings show nothing.
2. Keyboard only: Tab to the tab strip, Left/Right/Home/End move between tabs and change the list; Tab moves into the list.
3. Tick "Show the distance to the nearest one on the map": a dashed circle appears around the shaded area; change tab, the circle changes; untick, it disappears.
4. Turn JavaScript off: all lists are visible one under another, no tab strip, no checkbox.
5. Screen reader: tabs announce "tab, 1 of N, selected"; the note under the checkbox is read when the circle changes.
6. View source: no latitude/longitude of any place; `data-rings` has only ids, labels and radii.
