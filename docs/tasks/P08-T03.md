## Behaviour
- **GET**: the listing must be public (404 otherwise), the guest an active user who is not the owner (403). The stay comes from `getQuote` (the quote API's own function). Bad or missing parameters, or a stay that cannot be booked (rules, nights taken meanwhile), redirect to the listing page with the reason as a flash message. Otherwise the page shows: listing and stay (dates, check-in/out times, nights, guests), how booking works (instant, or "held for you for 24 hours while the host answers"), cancellation policy text, house rules, the final price breakdown exactly as `pricing.quote` returned it, the total, and the simulation banner. The form carries `in`, `out`, `guests`, `expectedTotalMinor` (the total shown), `idempotencyKey` (32 random hex characters, new on every page view) and `previousLines`. Responses are `no-store`.
- **POST**: refuses (redirect to the listing) a request without a whole-number `expectedTotalMinor`: the server always has a total to compare with. Then `reservations.create({ guest, listing, in, out, guests, expectedTotalMinor, idempotencyKey })`. Success (also a replay of the same key, i.e. a double click): flash with a reference (last 8 characters of the reservation id) and redirect to `confirmationPath()`; the wording differs for instant booking ("You are booked") and host approval ("sent to the host, who has 24 hours to answer").
- **Price changed** (`PriceChangedError`): nothing is booked. The page is drawn again with status 409: the previous and new total, the difference, the changed lines marked in words ("changed", not only colour), the new total as `expectedTotalMinor`, and a checkbox that must be ticked before the button works. The idempotency key stays (nothing was created under it).
- **Nights taken meanwhile** (`nights_unavailable`): back to the listing with a message. **A booking rule that changed** (notice, cut-off, stay length: a `ValidationError` with its own code): back to the listing with its message. **Key reused for a different stay**: back to a fresh checkout. Anything else (bad key format, 422 no price, 5xx) goes to the normal error pages.
- **Double click**: the button turns off after the first click (`checkout.js`); if two requests still arrive, the idempotency key makes them one booking (tested at service level and, with a real database, in `checkout.db.test.js`).
- **Session ended while the form was open**: logging in again creates a new session and a new CSRF token, so the open form would hit the global CSRF check and a dead-end 403. For this one route the CSRF middleware now calls `recoverCheckout`, which redirects to `GET /listings/:id/checkout?in&out&guests` (parameters only if they match strict date/number patterns; the id only if it is 24 hex characters). That page asks the guest to log in if needed and returns to the checkout with a fresh form and key. The rejected request books nothing. Every other route keeps the 403.
- **The total shown is the total stored**: the page's total, `expectedTotalMinor`, `reservations.create`'s own price check and `pricingSnapshot.totalMinor` all come from `pricing.quote`. `checkout.test.js` runs quote API -> checkout page -> form post -> real reservation service (in-memory fakes) and compares the stored snapshot total with the quote, for host-approval and instant listings, plus the "host raises the price in between" case.

## Deviations and limits
- **After booking, the guest lands on the listing page with a flash message**, because `/trips/:id` does not exist yet (P09-T01). Change `confirmationPath()` in `src/controllers/checkout.js` when it does.
- **Cancellation texts are new product decisions.** The roadmap only gives flexible's rule (full refund until 24 h before check-in). I wrote moderate (full refund until 5 days before, half until 24 h before) and strict (half refund at least 7 days before) in `src/config/cancellationText.js`. P09-T03's `refundFor` must implement exactly these, or both must be changed together; a guest reads this text before booking. Refunds are not calculated yet and no money moves.
- The csrf route-table test (`tests/integration/csrf.test.js`) had to change: it expected a 403 for every state-changing route; for the checkout POST it now expects the documented redirect.
- The recovery's flash message is lost when the guest is logged out (the redirecting GET consumes it and the login page shows "You must be logged in"); the flow itself still works.
- Requests for an unpublished listing, own listing, suspended account: 404 / 403 pages (no friendlier redirect).
- No rate limit on the POST beyond the global ones.
