## Behaviour
Order inside a pass: **expire → complete → orphans → drafts**. A failing step or reservation is recorded in `errors` and does not stop the rest; `runOnce` never throws.

1. **Expire.** `status:"pending"`, `holdExpiresAt < now` → `transition(id, "expired", { actor:"system", now })` (compare-and-set; releases the nights; history entry with actor `system`). A 409 (someone else won: another instance, or the guest cancelled) counts as `skipped`.
2. **Complete.** `status:"confirmed"`, check-out up to "today" in the earliest-ahead zone (query narrowing), then the exact test per reservation in `listingSnapshot.timezone` (default Asia/Kolkata): `transition(id, "completed")`. Completed reservations **keep** their ledger rows (ADR-004 "past rows kept").
3. **Orphan ledger rows.** A row is an orphan when its `reservation` source is missing or in `declined/expired/cancelled_*`, or its `block` source has no `AvailabilityBlock`. Only rows older than **10 minutes** (`orphanGraceMs`) are judged: booking inserts nights first and the owner afterwards (ADR-004), so a young row without an owner is normal. The scan is a rolling window of 1000 rows per pass resuming after the last `_id` (per instance, in memory), so a pass never reads the whole ledger. Rows are deleted by `_id`.
4. **Drafts.** `deleteStaleDrafts(Listing, { now, days: 90, destroy })`, at most once an hour per instance (`npm run sweep` forces it).

Paging: steps 1–2 read 200 reservations per page, 5 pages per pass, paged by `_id`; a reservation that cannot be moved this minute never hides the ones behind it. A bigger backlog continues on the next pass.

Multi-instance: no lock. Steps 1–2 are CAS, step 3 is delete-by-id (idempotent), step 4 re-checks each draft in its own delete.

## Deviations
- The draft clean-up runs at most hourly, not every minute (one query a minute for something that changes in months buys nothing). `npm run sweep` forces it.
- Orphan detection also treats rows of a reservation that has *released* its nights (declined/expired/cancelled) as orphans, not only rows of a missing reservation; that is invariant (c) of `tests/helpers/invariants.js` and ADR-004 ("missing or terminal"), and it lets the sweeper finish a release that failed half-way. Rows of `completed` reservations are **not** orphans.
- Roadmap says "complete confirmed where check-out date passed": done, but ledger rows of completed stays are kept, so P07-T08's invariant "(c) no ledger row belongs to a terminal reservation" must exclude `completed` (or this must change to deleting those rows). Decide in P07-T08.

## Limitations
- The orphan scan cursor and the draft timer live in memory per process; after a restart the scan starts from the beginning (harmless).
- A pending request whose hold has run out stays `pending` for up to one interval (60 s default) until the sweeper runs; its nights are takeable immediately (ledger stale-hold takeover), as T05 documented. After the pass the reservation is `expired` and consistent.
- The sweeper does not notify anyone of an expiry (notifications are P11) and does not change `paymentStatus` (simulated payments are P16).
- The timer is not stopped on shutdown (it is `unref`ed, so it never keeps the process alive).
