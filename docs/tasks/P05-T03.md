## Decisions
- Offset paging (`skip`/`limit`). With a stable sort it is correct and simple; a listing created while someone pages can shift a result by one. Keyset paging stays a later option (the roadmap asks only to consider it).
- A page past the end is a normal 200 page with an explanation and a link back, as the card says; a malformed page (`abc`, `0`, `1.5`) is treated as page 1.
- Price sorts: a listing without a price sorts first in "low to high". Only unmigrated legacy listings can lack one (flagged `needsReview`), and publishing needs a price, so I left it.
- `/listings` now answers the Q35 filter parameters (`destination`, `minPrice`, `guests`, ...) by their Q35 names. Task 36 renames and extends them; the sort and page parameters keep their names.
