## Behaviour
- **States** (colour, a letter, and always words): request awaiting your answer (R), booked (B), stayed (S, a completed stay), blocked by you (X); anything else is an unmarked free cell. Every occupied cell carries a visually hidden sentence, e.g. "Tuesday 3 November: booked, guest Ravi". Free cells are left empty for screen readers.
- **Click-through:** a booked, requested or stayed night links to `/trips/:id` (the booking page, which the host may open); a blocked night links to that listing's availability calendar for the same month. A night whose reservation cannot be found (or is not this host's) is shown as booked without a link or a guest name.
- **Free nights:** a request hold that ran out counts as free, the same rule as the per-listing calendar and the quote.
- **Months:** this month by default; an invalid `month` falls back to this month; older than 12 months or past the booking horizon is clamped (as in the per-listing calendar). Previous/next disappear at the ends.
- **Dates:** date-only values (ADR-003); "today" is the date in Asia/Kolkata.
- Every title is escaped (test with markup in a title).

## Limitations
- Read only: blocking nights and answering requests stay on their own pages.
- One month at a time; no week view, no drag, no guest search.
- Only the first 50 listings are shown (with a note).
- A multi-night stay appears as one cell per night (no merged bar), each linking to the same booking.
- The overview tiles of P13-T01 (task 62) are not built; this page does not depend on them.
