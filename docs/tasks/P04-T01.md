## Decisions
- **Price floor INR 100 / ceiling INR 10 lakh a night.** Validators run only on changed fields, so an old listing under the floor can still be edited; a new price under it is rejected (form and model).
- **Defaults are placeholders** for old listings: 2 guests, 1 bedroom/bed/bathroom, 1 to 30 nights, check-in 14:00, check-out 11:00, "moderate" policy, Asia/Kolkata (ADR-008). `needsReview` marks the guessed address, not these.
- **No invented price:** a listing with no usable price gets no `nightlyMinor`; the pages show "Price not set".
- Old `image` stays (cover) next to `images[]` until the media service (P04-T03).
