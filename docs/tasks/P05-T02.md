## What changed
| File | Change |
|---|---|
| `src/validation/searchQuery.js` (new) | `parseListingQuery(req.query, { now, timeZone })` -> `{ state, form, errors, searchParams, stay, page }`. ## Limitations / deviations
- The roadmap lists `amenities[]`; the page writes repeated `amenities=a&amenities=b` (Express reads that as a list) and accepts `amenities[]` too.
- `sort` and `minRating` are validated and kept in every link but do not change results yet (sorting is P05-T03, ratings need P12-T02). Results are newest first.
- The date filter excludes a listing for a taken night only; booking rules such as minimum nights and advance notice arrive with P07-T06. The list of unavailable ids is sent to MongoDB as `$nin`, fine for now, to be reworked if the ledger grows large.
- Paging is already here (12 per page); P05-T03 adds sorting and stable ordering.
